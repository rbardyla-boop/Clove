# Detox Season — Evidence Audit Ledger

Opened: 2026-08-23
Scope: 26 unpublished detox-season essays
Publication state: `DRAFTED / NOT FINAL-FROZEN`

## Rule

No essay becomes final because it sounds right, matches the thesis, or already contains a URL.

For each material factual claim record:

`CLAIM / STATUS / BEST SOURCE / COUNTEREVIDENCE / DATE-SENSITIVITY / WHAT WOULD CHANGE OUR MIND / ACTION`

Allowed claim status:

- `ESTABLISHED`
- `PROBABLE`
- `HYPOTHESIS`
- `PERSONAL EXPERIENCE`
- `UNSUPPORTED`
- `CUT`

A URL to a homepage is not the same thing as a source. Recover the exact article, paper, statute, table, archive snapshot or report where reasonably possible.

When deletion or scrubbing is plausible, current-web absence is insufficient. Check archived pages, historical URL paths, PDFs, mirrors and contemporaneous references.

## Completion codes

- `P0` — not audited
- `P1` — claim inventory complete
- `P2` — sources checked
- `P3` — counterevidence checked
- `P4` — copy repaired
- `FROZEN` — ready for final season packet

## Known guardrails to preserve during audit

### 01 — WHERE DID THE BOYS GO?

Current draft direction is allowed to say the gap is real. It is **not** yet allowed to say one ideological or institutional cause explains it.

Known research anchors to recover exactly:

- NCES enrollment sex breakdowns;
- BLS recent-high-school-graduate college enrollment data;
- historical reversal in total U.S. college enrollment around the late 1970s/early 1980s.

Audit question:

> What changed first, when did it change, and which proposed causes occurred too late to explain the beginning?

### 02 — THE MARKET FOUND LOOKSMAXXING

Known anchors requiring exact URL/provenance check:

- 2026 reporting on mainstream brands using `maxxing` / `mogging` language;
- ISD analysis of lexical migration from incel/blackpill spaces into generalized self-improvement language;
- Ozan Félix Sousbois, *Feminist Media Studies* (2026), on bodily suffering and looksmaxxing.

Boundary:

> Ordinary exercise, skincare, grooming and clothes are not the pathology being criticized. The target is the extreme claim that human worth is reducible to appearance/rank.

### 04 — DO MEN SOMETIMES NEED MEN?

Known anchor:

- Lanni et al. 2026 scoping review, PubMed PMID `42464623`.

Current safe interpretation:

- gender concordance evidence is mixed overall;
- some more consistent benefit appears in particular male substance-use settings;
- this does not justify mandatory same-sex care.

### 07/08 — DENOMINATOR + CROWD

Audit separately:

- availability/exemplar effects;
- base-rate neglect;
- visible social-proof counts;
- pluralistic ignorance/false consensus;
- evidence that audiences can infer selection bias;
- evidence denominator corrections sometimes have small effects.

Do not merge these mechanisms merely because they produce similar rhetoric.

### 09 — DEMOCRACY, MY ASS

Primary-source priority:

- Constitution text;
- Convention/ratification records;
- Federalist and Anti-Federalist material;
- founding-era state franchise rules.

Required red-team:

> Try to falsify the claim that the constitutional system gave legitimacy to the people while deliberately filtering direct popular control.

### 13 — OPTIMIZE ME

This article must be current-law safe at final freeze. Canadian political data/privacy rules can change. Final audit must date every legal claim and distinguish:

- Elections Canada list data;
- party-collected data;
- commercially acquired data;
- inferred data;
- ad-registry obligations;
- privacy/opt-out/oversight gaps.

### 14 — MICROTARGETING

Mandatory split:

- LISTENING
- PREDICTION
- PERSUASION
- OPTIMIZATION
- PRIVACY
- DEMONSTRATED EFFECTIVENESS

Do not let Cambridge Analytica mythology substitute for measured effect.

### 17 — WEF

Digital-archaeology standard is mandatory.

Need:

- original clip/source;
- exact words/context;
- date;
- numerical claim reconstructed step by step;
- what the clip establishes;
- what the math/inference does **not** establish;
- archived provenance if current source changed/disappeared.

### 18 — GOVERNMENT LIES

Each example is its own case. Evidence that one government lied once does not prove a different claim, actor or institution is lying now.

Required sentence-level discipline:

> Real institutional dishonesty lowers warranted trust. It does not eliminate the burden of proof for the next allegation.

### 23 — SCHOOL

Known guardrail:

- single-sex schooling by itself should not be presented as established remedy;
- controlled/meta-analytic evidence has generally found trivial or no broad advantage once selection/confounding is addressed.

Recover exact primary/meta-analysis sources in audit.

### 24 — EIGHTEEN

Prohibited shortcut:

> "The brain is not fully developed until 25."

Use developmental trajectories and task/domain specificity instead of a magical age switch.

## Freeze rule

An essay earns `FROZEN` only when:

- every material external claim is accounted for;
- weak links are replaced or explicitly labeled;
- counterevidence is not hidden;
- current-law/current-data claims are fresh enough for the scheduled date;
- the final prose matches the evidence status;
- the source packet is re-hashed after the final edit.

No bulk "looks good" approval.
