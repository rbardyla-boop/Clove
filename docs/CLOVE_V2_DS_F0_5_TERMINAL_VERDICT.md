## Claim under test

Whether CloveLearn can define an evidence-bounded Digital Stewardship foundation and action-first adult curriculum without importing fear-mongering, unsupported commercial claims, culture-war assertions, legal conclusions, shame, or unsafe account/device practices.

## What passed

- DS-P01 through DS-P12 adjudicated individually.
- Unsupported claims were narrowed, retired, or retained only as explicit hypotheses.
- DS-00 through DS-06 converted to `THREAT → WHY → ONE ACTION → CHECK → RECOVER` drills.
- Every drill has observable completion rather than passive reading.
- Internal adversarial review replayed fear, shame, legal/evasion, privacy, recovery, and action-completeness boundaries.
- Four wording defects found during adversarial review were repaired before freeze.
- No public product code or deployment was added by this unit.

## Disclosed limits

- No independent external evaluator replayed the exact packet.
- No human usability study proves that the eventual user interface is understandable without assistance.
- No evidence establishes long-term behaviour change or downstream health, financial, relationship, or security outcomes from these drills.
- Privacy rules are design contracts until an implementation-level telemetry/storage test proves them.
