## Claim 6 — network participation proves WEF control

STATUS: `UNSUPPORTED`

The evidence above establishes selection into an elite network, continuing alumni/community relationships and cabinet-level participation by some alumni.

It does not by itself establish:

- secret command authority;
- contractual loyalty to WEF;
- policy orders from Schwab;
- foreign-agent status;
- government policy being dictated by WEF.

A causal/control claim would require additional evidence: communications, directives, agreements, financial/control relationships, testimony or other records linking network membership to specific commanded government decisions.

## Strongest counterevidence / alternative interpretation

STATE: `P3 PARTIAL`

The strongest non-conspiratorial interpretation is not that the network is imaginary. It is that the WEF deliberately selects already-rising people, gives them networking/leadership opportunities, then points to their later success as evidence of the programme's influence and prestige. Under that model, cabinet penetration can reflect elite selection + networking + career success without implying command/control.

The strongest criticism of the essay itself would be:

> Focusing on the boast may overstate its operational meaning if `penetrate` was rhetorical language for successful alumni placement rather than an admission of control.

The essay currently handles that risk reasonably well by separating network participation from control.
