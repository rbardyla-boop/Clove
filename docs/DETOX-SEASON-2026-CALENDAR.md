# BATCH RULES

Every scheduled essay should contain:

- a strong first 5–10 lines;
- one memorable line worth quoting alone;
- a bounded claim rather than a totalizing theory;
- explicit counterevidence where it matters;
- a `BORING RECEIPT` section when external facts are material;
- AI disclosure;
- no paid-subscription pitch;
- no promise that Ryan will reply while offline;
- no engagement-bait question that requires monitoring comments.

## Source freeze rule

Time-sensitive factual claims should be sourced to records available before the detox begins. Do not schedule claims dependent on future events, unpublished government policy, elections, court decisions, market prices or unfolding news.

If a future development would materially change an essay, either remove the time-sensitive claim or do not schedule the essay.

## Detox integrity rule

The publishing machine is allowed to keep running. Ryan is not required to watch it run.

The point of the experiment is not to disappear socially while secretly refreshing analytics through another door.

No planned post should require checking views, likes, comments, KDP, X or Substack analytics during the detox.
