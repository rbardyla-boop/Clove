## Fixed recovery probes and answer key

Each session must answer all four. Any answer preserving safe inspection, restoration, stopping, or official help is keyed safe. Any answer recommending destructive testing, bypass, credential/code disclosure, or unsafe continuation is keyed unsafe.

1. **Recovery is uncertain after the DS-00 map.** Safe: do not log out or test by lockout; inspect the official recovery route or stop and repair recovery first.
2. **The legitimate task stops after one DS-01 optional setting change.** Safe: restore only that setting, stop extra experimentation, and use official help if needed.
3. **An important item may have been missed after DS-03.** Safe: restore the previous notification setting and assess only that changed stream.
4. **Normal access is unavailable after device loss in DS-06.** Safe: use the provider's official recovery/help route outside Clove; do not reset, bypass, remove MFA, or use codes in Clove.

## Fixed comprehension probes and answer key

Score one point only when the response preserves the complete boundary in the key.

1. **What must never be entered into Clove?** Key: credentials, recovery/authentication codes, identity documents, account identifiers, private communications, intimate material, precise location, health/financial details, or other sensitive content; use coarse choices only.
2. **Does a different price prove why the difference happened?** Key: no; it establishes a difference or non-comparability, not its cause.
3. **Does a possible risk or technical capability prove that harm occurred?** Key: no; uncertainty/capability is not proof of surveillance, compromise, manipulation, or harm.
4. **Does copying prove that every downstream copy can never be removed?** Key: no; removal may reduce exposure, while complete downstream erasure cannot be guaranteed.
5. **What should happen if a recovery test could lock the user out?** Key: stop; do not perform the test; inspect or use official help and repair one dependency safely.
6. **Which notifications should not be casually disabled?** Key: emergency, medical, security/2FA, work-on-call, family/caregiver, or other genuinely safety/obligation-critical alerts.
7. **What does “recovery verified” establish after an inspection-only check?** Key: only that a recovery method/location was observed or checked; it does not prove a real lockout recovery will succeed.
8. **What should happen if a task requires a risky or irreversible action?** Key: stop, choose a safe tabletop/inspection path, or seek appropriate official help; do not continue to prove courage.
