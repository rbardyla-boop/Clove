# Clove Temperance — Idea and Research Ledger

Status: WORKING CANON / NOT A CLAIM OF ESTABLISHMENT
Date: 2026-08-22

Purpose: move the movement's intellectual workshop out of chat and into a durable repository record.

This file intentionally stores ideas at different maturity levels. Inclusion here does **not** mean Clove endorses an idea as true.

## Classification

- `STORY` — Ryan's lived experience or memory. Not population evidence.
- `OBSERVATION` — something noticed in life, media, institutions, or public discussion.
- `HYPOTHESIS` — causal or explanatory idea that requires testing.
- `RESEARCH QUESTION` — question suitable for structured investigation.
- `ESTABLISHED` — supported well enough for public factual use, with sources maintained elsewhere.
- `POST CANDIDATE` — publication concept.
- `EARWORM` — memorable public-language candidate; rhetorical, not evidence.
- `HOLD` — potentially useful but too weak, risky, private, or underspecified for public use.

## Governing rules

1. Ryan talks. The receipts keep Ryan honest.
2. Exaggerate the picture. Never exaggerate the receipt.
3. No inherited guilt. No inherited innocence.
4. Equal worth does not require identical treatment.
5. The person may overrule the category.
6. A useful judgment must remain capable of being proved wrong.
7. No theory, credential, institution, movement, or founder gets tenure in being right.
8. Do not break one spell by casting a louder spell.
9. Public shock comes from plain speech and self-implication, not from making causal claims bigger than the evidence.
10. Clove does not ask for money for movement editorials, core tools, evidence ledgers, or corrections.

# B. Men and help-seeking

### B-001
Type: `ESTABLISHED / PUBLIC POLICY CONTEXT`

Canada is developing its first federal Men and Boys' Health Strategy for fall 2026, and current federal materials identify distinct male health challenges, including shorter life expectancy, higher suicide and premature mortality, lower use of primary/preventive care, and heavy male representation in opioid-related deaths.

Source handling: see `TEMPERANCE-MOVEMENT-LAUNCH.md` and the source URLs maintained there.

### B-006

# J. Productive friction / agency atrophy

### J-001
Type: `HYPOTHESIS`

**Friction Collapse Hypothesis:** humans naturally avoid effort, and modern technology/institutions increasingly remove cognitive, social, and practical friction. Some removed friction was useless suffering; some may have been practice that built competence.

### J-002
Type: `HYPOTHESIS`

**Agency atrophy:** if fewer tasks require navigation, memory, repair, boredom tolerance, uncertainty, rejection, conflict, or consequence management, fewer repetitions may be available for competence and confidence to develop.

Boundary: metaphor/hypothesis, not a diagnosed condition.

### J-003
Type: `MOVEMENT PRINCIPLE`

> The tool can perform the task without necessarily training the person.

### J-004
Type: `MOVEMENT PRINCIPLE`

### J-005
Type: `CAUSAL SPINE CANDIDATE`

> Productive friction -> competence -> confidence -> agency -> responsibility.

Status: conceptual model requiring empirical decomposition.

# S. Stop conditions

Do not promote a ledger item from idea to public factual claim when:

- the source cannot be located;
- the claim is stronger than the source;
- personal memory is being presented as documentary fact;
- a group pattern is being treated as an individual verdict;
- motive must be guessed;
- chronology contradicts the proposed cause;
- the proposed explanation cannot lose;
- the strongest counterevidence has not been inspected;
- a named private person would bear disproportionate harm relative to public-interest value;
- the material is primarily anger looking for a statistic.

The governing question remains:

> **Show me what would prove you wrong.**
