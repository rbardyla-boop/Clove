STEP 1 OF 6 • IDENTIFY
THE PROBLEM BEHAVIOR
Name the specific behavior you want to analyze. Not a pattern — one specific incident. Concrete, observable, precise. The clearer you are here, the more useful the chain.
NOT: "I self-destructed again."
YES: "Tuesday night, 11pm, I drank a fifth of whiskey alone after my wife went to bed."

STEP 3 OF 6 • THE SPARK
THE PROMPTING EVENT
This is the ONE thing that started the chain — the match that lit the tinder. It's not "everything." It's the specific moment the trouble began. Often smaller than you expect.
Examples: "She said 'you never listen.'" / "The bill notification came through." / "I drove past the bar." / "He didn't respond to my text for 3 hours."

WHY DID THIS HIT SO HARD?
What does this event mean to you? What story did your brain tell about it?
STEP 4 OF 6 • THE PATH
LINKS IN THE CHAIN
Walk the path between the trigger and the behavior. Each link is a thought, feeling, body sensation, or action that led to the next. Start right after the trigger. End just before the behavior. Aim for 3–8 links.
Link types: Thought — "No one cares anyway." | Feeling — Rage, 8/10 | Body — Chest tight, couldn't breathe | Action — Got in car, drove to liquor store
STEP 5 OF 6 • THE LEDGER
CONSEQUENCES
What did the behavior give you in the short run? What did it cost you? The ledger has to be honest on both sides — otherwise you won't understand why your brain keeps choosing this.
IMMEDIATE PAYOFF
What did it give you right away? (Relief, numbness, control, escape, connection, power…)
SHORT-TERM AFTERMATH
Hours/days after. What hit? (Guilt, shame, hangover, blowback, regret, physical effects…)
LONG-TERM COST
What is this pattern eroding? (Relationships, self-respect, goals, health, trust, mission…)
STEP 6 OF 6 • DEBRIEF
SOLUTION ANALYSIS
Now you break the chain. Which link was the weakest? What skill would have changed the outcome? What do you owe repair on? Commit to one concrete change.
WHICH LINK COULD YOU HAVE BROKEN?
Look at your chain. One or two links were the pivot points — where a different choice would have redirected everything.
