ANXIETY / PTSD COUNTERMEASURE
EXPOSURE HIERARCHY
ⓘ
Exposure therapy is the gold standard for anxiety and PTSD. Build your fear ladder: rank situations by distress (SUDS 0–100), face them gradually, log each exposure. Anxiety habituates — it literally cannot stay at peak forever. The data proves it. Work with your clinician to determine pacing. (Foa & Kozak 1986; Powers et al. 2010)
