MINDFULNESS

1. OBSERVE
Just notice. No judgment. No labeling. No fixing.

What do you see right now? What do you hear? What sensations are in your body?
Watch thoughts like clouds passing. Watch urges like waves rising and falling.
Stay with the raw data. Do not add commentary.
60
2. DESCRIBE
Put words on what you observed — only facts, no interpretation.

“I notice my chest is tight.”
“I hear my heart beating fast.”
“There is a thought: ‘I can’t handle this.’”
Say it silently or out loud. Keep it precise.
45
3. PARTICIPATE
Throw yourself fully into the present moment with intention.

One action. One breath. One step. Whole body, whole mind.
No half-in. No multitasking. No escape.
Be here. Completely. Right now.
90
