Your brain is running a threat program that isn't yours.
Three steps. MA protocol. Shut it down.

STEP 01 OF 03
NAME THE THREAT
What is your brain telling you right now? Write the threatening interpretation — the worst-case story your mind is running.
"I can't handle this." "This is going to destroy everything." "I'm going to fail and everyone will see."
Say it clearly. Get it out of your head and onto the screen.
STEP 02 OF 03
DEMAND OR CHALLENGE?
Your brain is framing this as a demand — something impossible, something you can't survive.

But is it actually impossible? Or is it a challenge — tough, uncomfortable, maybe painful — but something you have the resources to handle?

Write what makes this a challenge, not a death sentence. What resources, experience, or people do you have?
STEP 03 OF 03
THE REFRAME
Now rewrite the threat as a statement you actually believe. Not toxic positivity. A realistic, earned reframe.
"I've been through worse. This will be tough but I can handle it."
"This is hard, but I have [specific resource/person/skill] and I've survived [specific past challenge]."
Make it specific. Make it true. Make it yours.
