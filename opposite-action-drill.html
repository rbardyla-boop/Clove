OPPOSITE ACTION
⚠
THIS IS A SELF-HELP TOOL — NOT CLINICAL DBT. Opposite Action is adapted from Dialectical Behavior Therapy emotion regulation skills. This tool does not replace professional treatment with a licensed therapist. If you're in crisis, call or text 988.
STEP 1 OF 4
◈
THE PRINCIPLE: When your emotion is pushing you to act in a way that will make things worse — you act in the exact opposite direction. All the way. Not halfway. The emotion loses power when you stop feeding it its preferred behavior.

STEP 3 OF 4 • THE AUDIT
CHECK THE FACTS
Before you act — or before you analyze why you acted — check the facts. Two questions. Answer honestly. This determines whether you need Opposite Action or just to validate your emotion.
Is your emotion justified by the actual facts?
Justified means: something actually happened that warrants this emotion. Not justified means: your interpretation added a story that isn't supported by facts.
✓
JUSTIFIED
The facts support this emotion. Something real happened.
◌
NOT JUSTIFIED
My brain added a story. The facts don't fully support this.
NOTE — JUSTIFIED EMOTION
You can still use Opposite Action.
Even when an emotion is justified, the action urge may still cause harm. Anger at betrayal is justified. Burning down the relationship is not. Opposite Action is about consequences, not whether the emotion is "right."
