KEEP ACCOUNTS APART.

Learn how to spot important and low-risk account lanes without moving an important account.

Why this helps: One problem may do less harm when important accounts are not all tied together. Avoid: do not move an important account just to test this lesson.
Start

Ready to map your account lanes?

Overview DS-00 DS-01 DS-02 DS-03 DS-04 DS-05 DS-06

Private by design: Never type an email address, provider name, username, phone number, password, recovery code, or account identifier into Clove. This drill stores only coarse choices on this device and sends no drill answers anywhere.

First-run safety: Do not move or change the sign-in or recovery route for banking, government, health, password-manager recovery, primary work, or another high-consequence account during this drill. A secondary email or alias does not make you anonymous. Do not log out, reset a password, change recovery email or phone, remove multi-factor authentication, or use a backup code just to test this drill.

Why stop here: A second account is not always safer. More accounts can mean more recovery work. This lesson only maps what you already have; it does not ask you to change an account.
