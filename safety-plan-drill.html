SAFETY PLAN
⚠
THIS IS A SELF-HELP TOOL — NOT A CLINICAL SAFETY PLAN. If you are in immediate danger, call 911, go to your nearest emergency room, or call/text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). This tool helps you organize your plan — it does not replace professional safety planning with a licensed therapist.
STEP 1 OF 7
Warning Signs
What do you notice when things start going sideways? These are your early indicators — the feelings, thoughts, situations, or behaviors that tell you a crisis might be building.
Examples: "I stop answering texts." "I start picking fights." "I can't sleep for 2+ nights." "I start thinking about drinking." "Everything feels pointless."
0/2000
STEP 2 OF 7
Internal Coping
What can you do on your own — without contacting anyone — to take your mind off the crisis or resist the urge? These are your solo tools.
Examples: "Box breathing for 4 minutes." "Go for a walk — doesn't matter where." "Cold water on my face (TIPP)." "Write what I'm feeling." "Push-ups until I can't."
0/2000
STEP 3 OF 7
Distraction
People you can contact and places you can go — not to talk about the crisis, but just to be around someone or somewhere that takes you out of your head.
Examples: "Call my brother — talk about literally anything." "Go to the gym." "Drive to the coffee shop and sit there." "Text my buddy about the game."
0/2000
STEP 4 OF 7
People to Ask for Help
People you trust enough to tell the truth to. These are the ones you call when it's real — when you need to say "I'm not okay" and mean it.
STEP 5 OF 7
Professionals & Crisis Lines
Your therapist, sponsor, counselor, crisis lines. The people trained for exactly this moment. Write their info here so you don't have to search for it when you need it most.
CRISIS LINES — ALWAYS AVAILABLE
988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988)
Crisis Text Line (text HOME to 741741)
Veterans Crisis Line (call 988, press 1)
Canada: Talk Suicide (1-833-456-4566)
Canada: Crisis Text Line (text HOME to 686868)
STEP 6 OF 7
Making the Environment Safe
What can you do to reduce access to things that could hurt you during a crisis? This is about removing or securing means — not permanently, but when the risk is high.
Examples: "Give my firearms to [name] when I'm in the red." "Move medications to [person]'s house." "Delete the dealer's number." "Put the car keys in the lockbox."
0/2000
STEP 7 OF 7
My Reason
The one thing — or the few things — that keep you here. Not what you think you should say. What actually works when it's 3 AM and you're in the garage. Write the real thing.
0/2000
