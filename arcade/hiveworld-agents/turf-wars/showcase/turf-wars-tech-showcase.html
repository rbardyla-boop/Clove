01

What this is — and is not

The engineering claim is narrow and checkable: territory outcomes are deterministic replays validated by a delegable one-operation fraud-proof, so peers agree without a referee. The product claim is deliberately absent.

● What it is

  • A research prototype of a peer-hosted territory game where authority is replay-determinism + a one-op fraud-proof, not a server.
  • Signed-op block logs, content-addressed snapshots, a deterministic attack simulator, a commit→reveal settlement, a cross-block beacon, a challenge window, and a convergent overlay with keyless fraud-proof revocation.
  • 24 zero-dependency lab modules, 9 evidence/stress packs, exercised by 146/146 turf-wars tests within a 1297/1297 unit suite.

● What it is not

  • Not live, not public, not minors-facing. No one can play it against anyone.
  • No UGC, no accounts, no login, no chat, no free text, no uploads, no images.
  • No live P2P network, no peer discovery, no production runtime, no live world loader.
  • No economy: no transfer, cash-out, marketplace, rewards, or ownership. "Loss" is reversible cosmetic scorch only.
02

Architecture

Six deterministic layers. Every layer is pure and content-addressed; no layer holds central authority. The safety layer (Phase 4) is planned, not built, and is gated on legal counsel.

03

Settlement sequence

An attack is a two-phase commit. The reveal is checked by anyone re-running the pure simulator; a mismatch is a fraud-proof that any peer can verify without the defender online.

06

Safety boundary — what this does not do

The interesting product (a live, peer-hosted, possibly minors-accessible game) is exactly the part that is not built and not authorized. That separation is deliberate and load-bearing.

Hard boundary

  • No live game mode. LIVE_WORLD_LOADER_ENABLED stays false; nothing here flips it.
  • Lab-only / prod-denylisted. This file is under arcade/hiveworld-agents/; the curated client upload excludes the whole prefix. Turf-wars upload count: 0.
  • No legal clearance is claimed. This dossier is a technical record, not a compliance assessment, and is not legal advice.
  • Phase 0 counsel review is BLOCKING for any live or minors-facing use, and a charter-superseding ADR would be required — neither exists.
  • The lab proves the mechanism, not the policy. Whether a quorum + render-gate is a sufficient minors-safety control is a question for counsel, not a lab pass/fail.

Three release modes are defined in TURF_WARS_PHASE0_LIVE_MINORS_UNBLOCK_PACKET.md: lab-only (current), adults-only closed pilot, and minors-accessible public. Only lab-only is in effect. Moving past it needs explicit gates, e.g. AUTHORIZED: BUILD PHASE 4a — SAFETY QUORUM — LAB ONLY.
