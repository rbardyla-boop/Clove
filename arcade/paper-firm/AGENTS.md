# Paper Firm — First Shift implementation rules

This directory implements the field/rendering side of Paper Firm. Work continuously; do not stop for per-phase or per-commit approval.

## Non-negotiable laws

- Clients send intent. Authority writes reality.
- Clove owns only ephemeral field reality: movement, presence, zone occupancy, pickup/extract eligibility, and server-authored field receipts.
- RUG owns organizational reality: OBS, evidence, doctrine, ancestry, work, leases, packets, artifact state, proof, READY_TO_SIGN and SIGN.
- A field receipt is evidence, never knowledge.
- Do not create a second organizational authority in Clove.
- Two humans must be distinct authenticated principals in the acceptance run.
- No AFK XP or timer progress. Offline progress must come from real RUG events.

## Ballpoint art law

A still must read as a playable 3D world drawn in ruled exercise-book paper with one cheap blue ballpoint pen.

- warm ivory paper; faint school-book rules across the whole frame;
- medium ballpoint blue contour with human line-weight wobble;
- cheap low-poly geometry; depth/perspective is real;
- dense two-direction hatch on solids; paper rules remain visible under hatch;
- red ballpoint only for rejection/correction/authority/signature;
- yellow highlighter only for verified/held emphasis;
- repair = tape; reject = red X/stamp; verify = red check; ancestry = drawn arrows/threads;
- no PBR, photoreal materials, black/gray shading, bloom, normal maps, or beige post-process shortcut.

Mandatory blocker: if a hatched box hides the notebook rules, fix the renderer before modeling more world.
