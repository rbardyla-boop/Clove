CBT THOUGHT RECORD
STEP 1 OF 6
ⓘ
Clinical context: CBT thought records are a core tool in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (Beck, 1979). This is a self-help exercise — not therapy. If you're in crisis, use Red Protocol or Safety Plan.
STEP 01 — SITUATION
What Happened?
Describe the specific situation that triggered the thought. Not the story you told yourself about it — the raw facts. Who, what, where, when. Like a camera saw it.
Example: "My boss didn't respond to my email for 3 hours. I was at my desk. It was Tuesday afternoon."
STEP 02 — EMOTIONS
What Did You Feel?
Name the emotion. Not the thought — the raw feeling in your body. Rate its intensity. Be honest about how hard it hit.
INTENSITY
50
Moderate. Present and uncomfortable.
STEP 03 — AUTOMATIC THOUGHT
What Went Through Your Mind?
The hot thought. The first thing your brain said before you could filter it. Not what you think you should have thought — what actually fired.
Example: "He's ignoring me on purpose. I'm about to get fired. I'm not good enough for this job."
HOW MUCH DO YOU BELIEVE THIS THOUGHT RIGHT NOW?
75
Strong belief. Feels like fact.
STEP 04 — COGNITIVE DISTORTIONS
Name the Pattern
Your brain has favorite tricks. Select every distortion you notice in the thought you wrote. Most thoughts carry 2-3. Tap to select.
0 SELECTED
STEP 05 — EVIDENCE
Weigh the Case
Lawyer up. What actual evidence supports this thought? What evidence contradicts it? Facts only — not feelings dressed as facts.
EVIDENCE FOR
EVIDENCE AGAINST
Pro tip: "I feel like" is not evidence. "He said X on Tuesday" is. Look for facts you'd present in court, not feelings you'd share in a diary.
STEP 06 — BALANCED THOUGHT
Rewrite the Verdict
Now write a more balanced thought. Not positive thinking — accurate thinking. A thought that accounts for ALL the evidence, not just the prosecution's case.
Example: "He might be busy. Three hours isn't unusual. He replied to my last email same day. I have no evidence I'm being fired. I can follow up tomorrow if I haven't heard back."
HOW MUCH DO YOU BELIEVE THE ORIGINAL THOUGHT NOW?
30
Weakened. Evidence shifted the weight.
