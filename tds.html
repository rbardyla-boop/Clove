What this is
THE BOOK GETS A MEMORY
Pages vanish. Policies change. Old PDFs disappear. Viral screenshots lose their provenance. A printed book cannot update itself, so the evidence trail lives here. If the book is corrected, the correction stays visible. If an archived page changes a verdict, the verdict changes.
A society that cannot remember what its institutions said last year cannot audit what they tell it today.

Method
HOW A CLAIM SURVIVES
Current-web absence is not enough. When deletion or revision is plausible, the research pass checks live pages, Wayback snapshots, historical URLs, archived PDFs, mirrors, old rosters, contemporaneous reporting, citations to vanished material, and version history. A missing page can mean many things. It is not a confession.
The promise
IF I GOT IT WRONG, YOU SHOULD BE ABLE TO SHOW ME
The book's strongest rule is simple: the joke may imply; the evidence must prove. A claim the author likes does not get extra lives. A claim the author hates does not get buried because the people sharing it have terrible memes.
