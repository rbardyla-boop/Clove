What the hell is this

Tactical Cards.
No Therapy Speak.

CloveLearn is The Operator's Deck — a radically private, browser-only web app for men doing the real inner work. Not journaling apps. Not mood trackers. Not anything that requires you to call your feelings by their clinical names.

Created by Ryan Bardyla, built live with Grok and Claude. 115 tactical op cards in playing-card format, each paired with a song that hits the right nerve, each flipping to a 24-hour mission so you actually do something with the truth.

8 suits covering every corner of the work: self-deception, relationships, addiction, PTSD, the rebuild, and the brotherhood you need to not do it alone.

Every time your kid says "Dad, watch this" — that's a bid. A small request for connection. You get maybe 10,000 in a lifetime. Every grunt or "not now" is a declined bid.

What's inside

Every Feature
That Ships

[ 01 ] PRIVACY
Zero Servers
No login. No account. All data lives in your browser's localStorage. Delete the tab, delete the data. Your reflections never leave your device — full stop.
[ 02 ] SOUNDTRACK
Music Preference
Set your genre at onboarding: Pop, Alt/Indie, Classic/Old School, or Country. When a card's original track isn't your vibe, the deck surfaces a Spotify search for your kind of sound on the same theme.
[ 03 ] CHECK-IN
Field Protocol
7-question status check before you draw. Indirect behavioral probes across 6 domains — designed to defeat rehearsed "I'm fine" answers. Composite status routes your card accordingly.
[ 04 ] EVIDENCE
Mission Log
After completing a mission, log what actually happened. Not what you planned. Not what you wish. The evidence file builds over time — 115 missions of real data about who you're becoming.
[ 05 ] CRISIS
I'm Not Okay Mode
One button that immediately activates a breathing protocol, de-escalation text, and a direct link to the Therapist Finder. No judgment. No preamble. Just the thing you need when you need it.
[ 06 ] STUCK POINTS
CPT Drill
Embedded Cognitive Processing Therapy stuck-points worksheet. Write the thought, challenge it, rewrite it. Wired into the King cards and accessible in the More tab whenever you're spinning.
[ 07 ] FIELD MANUAL
Knowledge Library
41 evidence-based micro-lessons on the science behind every drill. DBT, CPT, ACT, safety planning — explained without clinical jargon. The why behind the what.
[ 08 ] ADHD OPS
10-Tool Field Kit
Dopamine Depot, Weekly Ops, Grove Ops, Quest Forge, Kanban, Prompt Depot, Micro Ops, Grind Ops, Body Double, Visual Planner. Executive function support built for brains that don't run on willpower.
[ 09 ] DIAGNOSTICS
System Check
Full data integrity audit. Scans all 52 localStorage keys, flags corruption, shows storage capacity, offers repair tools. The kind of thing most apps don't give you — visibility into your own data.
Honest Assessment

What Works.
What Doesn't.

What's genuinely great
  • Radical privacy in a world that monetises every feeling you have
  • The cards are sharp. Not vague affirmations — actual uncomfortable truths with a specific 24-hour action
  • FIELD PROTOCOL check-in is the right ritual. 90 seconds, 6 domains, routes your card to where you actually are
  • Soundtrack matching is a legitimately clever UX choice — music creates emotional state before the card lands
  • The voice is consistent. Someone who does this work wrote these, not a content team chasing keywords
  • Music Preference now lets you swap any song for your genre — Pop, Alt, Classic, or Country Spotify searches built-in
Room to grow
  • Still early — some suits feel thinner than others. Brotherhood and Rebuild deserve more depth
  • Export exists now in Tool Shed (text archive of all sessions) — but no cloud backup, which is by design
  • The 115-card count across 8 suits and 7 bonus cards
  • Mobile performance improved with v1.1 RAF batching — older phones still feel some weight on tab switches
  • No community layer yet. These cards work better when you know someone else is doing the same mission
