IMPROVE

I — Imagery
Create or recall a safe, peaceful scene in vivid detail.
Beach. Forest. Mountain ridge at sunrise. Your own internal bunker.
Stay in it until the wave passes.
M — Meaning
Find or create purpose in the pain right now.
“This is training.” “This is proof I can endure.” “This is the forge.”
Reframe. Re-purpose. Refuse to let it be meaningless.
P — Prayer
Speak to whatever force you respect — God, the universe, your future self, your dead brothers.
Ask for strength, clarity, or just to make it through the next 60 seconds.
R — Relaxing Actions
Progressive muscle relaxation, stretching, slow breathing, hot shower, weighted blanket.
Physically tell the body: the fight is over for now.
O — One thing in the moment
Laser focus on a single sensory input: sound of breath, feel of floor, taste of water.
Anchor. Ground. Refuse to let the mind run in circles.
V — Vacation (short)
Brief, deliberate break: 5–20 minutes of something that feels like escape.
Music. Walk. Lie on floor staring at ceiling. Legal, fast, effective.
E — Self-encouragement
Talk to yourself the way you’d talk to a brother in the same pain.
“You’ve survived worse.” “This ends.” “You’re still here.”
Say it. Mean it. Repeat.
