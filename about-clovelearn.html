THE SYSTEM
A Mission. Every Day.
Built for men who've tried the softer options. Check in. Get a card matched to where you actually are. Execute a 24-hour mission. Log evidence. Rank up. Works for addiction recovery, PTSD, ADHD, relationship repair — or just getting off the floor after a bad stretch.
1
Check in. 90 seconds across six domains. Honest answers route your card to where you actually are.
2
Draw and flip. A hard truth. Then your mission — something specific, something today. Press play on the matched track. Let it land.
3
Execute and log. Do the mission. Write what actually happened. One sentence is enough. That's where change gets recorded.
