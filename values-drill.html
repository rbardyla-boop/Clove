ACCEPTANCE & COMMITMENT THERAPY
VALUES CLARIFICATION
STEP 1 OF 5
ⓘ
Clinical context: Values clarification is a core ACT process (Hayes, Strosahl & Wilson, 2012). Values are directions, not destinations — you never "arrive." This drill helps you find yours and see where your actions don't match. Not therapy — a compass check.
STEP 01 — LIFE DOMAINS
What Matters to You?
Select the life domains that matter most to you right now. Not what should matter. Not what used to matter. What actually pulls at you when you're honest with yourself. Pick 3–6.
0 SELECTED (PICK 3–6)
STEP 02 — IMPORTANCE
How Much Does Each One Matter?
Rate how important each domain is to you on a scale of 1–10. Not how well you're doing — how much it matters. There's a difference.
STEP 03 — CURRENT ACTION
How Well Are You Living It?
Now rate how consistently your actual behavior aligns with each value over the past month. 1 = completely neglected. 10 = living it daily. The gap between importance and action is where the work is.
STEP 04 — GAP ANALYSIS
Where Are You Off Course?
The bigger the gap between importance and action, the more that domain is costing you. Green = aligned. Yellow = drifting. Red = misaligned.
COMMITTED ACTION
Pick the domain with the biggest gap. Write one specific, small, doable action you can take in the next 24 hours to move toward that value. Not a goal — a behavior.
