STEP 1 OF 8
FIELD INTEL
What happened?
Briefly describe the situation, event, or trigger. Facts only — not your interpretation yet. Two or three sentences is enough.
Example: "Got passed over for promotion again. Supervisor said I wasn't ready. Third year running."

STEP 2 OF 8
TARGET ACQUISITION
What's the locked belief?
Write the exact thought your brain is stuck on. This is the interpretation, not the event. Be specific. Start with "I am..." or "People always..." or "It was my fault that..."
Examples:
"I am fundamentally not good enough."
"If I trust people, I will get hurt."
"I have no control over anything that matters."
"It is my fault this happened to me."

STEP 3 OF 8
CASUALTY REPORT
What does it cost you emotionally?
Tap to select each emotion this thought triggers. Slide to rate intensity from 0 (barely there) to 100 (overwhelming). Select at least one.
STEP 4 OF 8
PATTERN RECOGNITION
Which core belief area is disrupted?
Prolonged stress and trauma tend to hit five specific areas of how we see ourselves and the world. Identifying your area tells you what you're actually challenging. Pick the one that fits best.
STEP 5 OF 8
EVIDENCE LOCKER
What does the actual record show?
List real facts and events — not feelings — on both sides. Your brain only files evidence that confirms what it already believes. Force it to give up the rest. Fill at least one side.

STEP 6 OF 8
BREACH AND CLEAR
Which thinking traps are running?
These are shortcuts your nervous system built to protect you. They worked once. Name them and they lose power. Select all that apply — or skip if none fit.
STEP 7 OF 8
NEW OPERATING TRUTH
What's the calibrated version?
Write a balanced alternative. Not toxic positivity. Not denial. A statement that accounts for evidence on both sides. It should feel true, just less extreme. A calibrated operator doesn't pretend everything is fine — he holds the full picture.
Not this: "Everything is fine."
This: "I've had real failures AND real wins. One outcome isn't a verdict."
Or: "I can't control everything. I can control what I do next."
Or: "I was hurt. That doesn't mean I'm broken or that it defines what happens next."

STEP 8 OF 8
AFTER ACTION
How intense are those emotions now?
After working through the evidence and building a balanced thought — re-rate the same emotions. The goal isn't zero. A reduction is progress. If some went up, that's also data worth logging.
