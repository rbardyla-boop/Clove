TIPP

1. TEMPERATURE
Cold water on face / hold ice pack to eyes & cheeks / splash cold water / step into cold shower.

Goal: Activate mammalian dive reflex — force heart rate & nervous system to drop fast.
30
2. INTENSE EXERCISE
30–60 seconds all-out effort: burpees, push-ups, sprint in place, wall sits, shadow boxing.

Burn the adrenaline. Exhaust the body. Starve the emotion.
45
3. PACED BREATHING
Inhale 4 seconds → hold 4 → exhale 6–8 seconds.
Repeat until calm returns. Nose in, mouth out if possible.
60
4. PROGRESSIVE RELAXATION
Tense → release each muscle group head to toe.
5–7 seconds tense → 10–15 seconds release.
90
