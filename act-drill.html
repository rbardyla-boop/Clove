STEP 1 OF 6
ORIENT THE COMPASS
What actually matters right now?
Not what you think should matter. Not what looked good last year. What would make you say "that was worth fighting for" at the end of today? Select up to 3.

STEP 2 OF 6
DEFINE THE MISSION
What does living by those values look like?
Not a goal. A quality of action. Not "be a better father" — but "showing up at dinner without checking my phone." What does it actually look like when you're on track?
Examples:
"Being present with my kids even when I'm exhausted. Not checking out when things get hard."
"Calling my brother back the same day. Not disappearing for weeks when I'm struggling."
"Telling my sponsor the real version, not the edited one."

STEP 3 OF 6
IDENTIFY THE SNAG
What thought is pulling you off course?
Write the exact sticky thought that's running in the background. The one your brain treats like a fact even though it might not be. Don't dress it up — write it raw.
Examples:
"I've already ruined it, so what's the point."
"I don't deserve to be in the room with people who have their lives together."
"If I try and it doesn't work, it confirms I'm the problem."
0 / 300
DEFUSION TECHNIQUE
Name it. Don't fight it.
The goal isn't to eliminate the thought — it's to stop treating it as a command. After writing it above, say it out loud with this prefix:
"I notice I'm having the thought that..."

That distance — between you and the thought — is where the choice lives.
STEP 4 OF 6
DROP THE STRUGGLE
How willing are you to carry the discomfort and act anyway?
This is not about feeling ready. You don't have to want the feeling — just be willing to have it while you do what matters. Rate your willingness right now.

STEP 5 OF 6
THE ACTUAL MOVE
One committed action. Today.
Specific. Time-bound. Values-aligned. Not "be a better person" — a thing you will actually do in the next 24 hours that moves toward what matters. If it's small, that's fine. Small and done beats perfect and theoretical.
Examples:
"Today at 1900 I will sit at the dinner table without my phone for the full meal."
"I will call my sponsor before I go to sleep tonight. Even if it's awkward. Even if it's just two minutes."
"I will write down three things I actually did right this week before I open the news."
