01
Dancing With The Devil
NON-FICTION · ADDICTION · RECLAMATION
The internet rewired billions of minds. This is the account of what it did to one, and how the same machine that built the trap became the tool that dismantled it. A first-person investigation into dopamine, digital addiction, and the slow work of reclamation — told without clinical distance, by someone who lost years to the loop and built his way out of it.
"You don't have to be evil to dance with the devil. You just have to not notice you're dancing."

02
Mathematics of the Heart
NON-FICTION · RELATIONSHIPS · CONNECTION
A forensic examination of what kills relationships slowly and invisibly — hedonic adaptation, bid decline, front-stage performance, and the accumulated cost of armor. Not a therapy book. A field manual for men who realize they are present in body and absent everywhere else, and want to do something about it before it's too late.
"Every time your kid says 'Dad, watch this' — that's a bid. A small request for connection. You get maybe 10,000 in a lifetime. Every grunt or 'not now' is a declined bid."

03
The Biological Bruise
NON-FICTION · ADHD · RSD · NEURODIVERGENCE
Forty-four years without a name for what was running in his nervous system. This is the account of a late ADHD diagnosis and the realization that the coping strategies that got him through — military service, hyper-productivity, social masking — were prosthetics for a brain that wasn't broken, just different. A manual for men who suspect the same thing about themselves.
"I am a man who spent forty-four years without a name for what was running in his nervous system. The name didn't fix anything. But it stopped me from blaming myself for the hardware."
The Biological Bruise · Ryan Bardyla

04
The Pendulum Swings
NON-FICTION · INSTITUTIONS · SYSTEMS · SOCIETY
An analysis of how institutions — media, politics, social platforms, educational systems — use behavioral conditioning at scale. The same manipulation mechanics that operate on individuals operate on populations. Understanding the architecture is the first defense against it. For men who sense something is wrong with the larger picture but haven't been able to name the system.
"The pendulum doesn't swing on its own. Someone is always pushing it. The question is whether you know who, and whether you're one of the ones getting hit."
The Pendulum Swings · Ryan Bardyla

05
The Grind: Retardmaxxing
NON-FICTION · DISCIPLINE · IDENTITY · RADICALIZATION
How algorithmic platforms weaponize the same behavioral conditioning methods used to build armies — and funnel men without identity anchors into ready-made ones. The book examines discipline culture, identity fusion, and the pipeline from self-improvement to radicalization. Not an argument against working hard. An argument for working on the right things, with eyes open about who benefits from your obedience.
"Building an organic personality is harder than downloading an ersatz one. It takes longer. It requires tolerating the very uncertainty that drove you to the ideology in the first place."
The Grind: Retardmaxxing · Ryan Bardyla
