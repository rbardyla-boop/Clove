Trust & safety
WHAT'S INSIDE
The desktop app is the exact same HTML files as the web version, wrapped in an Electron shell. No hidden code. No network calls. No telemetry. You can unzip the installer and read every file yourself.
YOUR DATA
All data stays in your user folder (%APPDATA% on Windows, ~/Library on Mac). Uninstalling the app does not delete your missions, evidence, or check-in history. Your data outlives the app.
SECURITY
Context isolation ON. Node integration OFF. Sandbox ON. The app can't access your files, network, or anything outside its own window. Camera blocked. Microphone pre-granted for future voice features (local only, never cloud).
