## Posting rules

1. Do not manufacture certainty for a stronger hook.
2. Keep Canada and U.S. claims separate when the evidence is jurisdiction-specific.
3. Distinguish measured effect from mechanism and mechanism from moral judgment.
4. Include counterevidence when it materially changes the story.
5. Link readers to the CloveLearn research project rather than asking them to trust the author.
6. Do not turn null results into failures of the project; they are results.
7. Preserve corrections publicly.
8. Keep the writing funny enough that a normal human can finish it.
