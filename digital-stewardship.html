Help somebody else

Good habits work better when people can ask without shame.

Make a pause normal

Agree on a phrase such as “call me before you send money.” Use a known route to check unusual requests.

  • Do not ask for someone’s secret to prove who they are.
  • Do not shame someone who was tricked.
  • Help them contact the bank or service through its official route.

Teach, do not take over

Show someone where the real setting or help page is. Let them keep control of their account and device.

  • Never ask them to send a password or code.
  • Use a trusted person or official support when the stakes are high.
  • Leave Clove out of private account details.
