Rhythm

Pulse Tap

Tap on the beat as the pulse hits the ring. Simple to start, tight to master.

The feel: 3·2·1 countdown, a hit burst on every clean tap, and an animated grade at the buzzer.

Lane runner

Signal Sprint

Slide between lanes, catch the pulses, dodge the noise. Speeds up the longer you last.

The feel: smooth lane lean, a live distance readout, and clear collect / miss feedback.

Memory

Neon Grid

Watch the grid light a path, then play it back. Each round the path grows.

The feel: every cell has its own tone, a brighter reveal, and a "your turn" cue before you tap.

Make · local only

Make your own arcade game

Build a small cabinet from simple controls, playtest it right in your browser, and share it as a local file or share code. It runs only on your device — there is no upload, and nothing is published to the live city or earns tickets.

About tickets

Playing well earns tickets inside the arcade. Tickets are a score — a way to track attention and skill, nothing more.

They are not money and not a coin. There is nothing to buy, nothing to deposit, no balance to cash out, and no trading. The whole experience is free to open and play.
