VOLUNTEER FIREFIGHTER — IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS

As a volunteer firefighter, your entitlements differ from professional firefighters:

• WSIB coverage: YES — through your municipality as a Schedule 1 employer. File Form 6 the same way a professional firefighter would.
• IAFF CBA / union grievance: NOT typically available — volunteers are not usually covered by IAFF local collective agreements.
• ESA (Employment Standards Act): Does NOT apply to volunteer firefighters (ESA Schedule 1 exclusion).
• FPPA s.51: Volunteer-specific provisions of the Fire Protection and Prevention Act apply to your service.
• PTSD presumption (WSIA s.15.1): Contact WSIB directly to confirm your volunteer status qualifies — the presumption covers "firefighters" but confirm Schedule 1 applicability.

This tool filters benefit results to show volunteer-relevant pathways only.
