# CP-CDTT v0.3.2 — Obligation Semantics and Claim Activity Contract

## Canonical activity source

`F2F3_CLAIM_ACTIVITY_SOURCE_v0.3.2.csv` is authoritative. Case-level claims, axis claims, evaluator rows, and gold profiles must be generated from it. They must not independently decide whether an axis is active.

`claim_activity` has exactly three values:

- `CLAIMED`: an affirmative claim is being adjudicated.
- `NEGATED`: an explicit negative or limiting claim is being adjudicated. This remains active.
- `NOT_CLAIMED`: no claim exists on that axis.

Only `NOT_CLAIMED` suppresses proof-obligation adjudication. A sentence such as “these mechanisms are not equivalent” is `NEGATED`, never `NOT_CLAIMED`.

## Deterministic obligation status

For every active obligation:

| Status | Exact condition |
|---|---|
| `PASS` | The frozen packet explicitly satisfies the obligation under the stated claim and regime. |
| `FAIL` | The frozen packet explicitly contradicts the obligation or supplies evidence that the obligation is not satisfied. |
| `UNKNOWN` | The obligation is applicable, but the frozen packet does not decide it. Missing evidence is not a pass. |
| `NOT_APPLICABLE` | The obligation is conditional, the condition is false for this claim, and the evaluator records that applicability reason. |

An active axis with a direct `FAIL` is `FAILED`. If it has no direct `FAIL` but has an applicable `UNKNOWN`, it is `BLOCKED`. Dependencies are applied only after the axis-local result. A dependency cannot turn a direct `FAILED` result into `BLOCKED`.

## Response invariants

- `NOT_CLAIMED` axis → zero proof-obligation rows.
- `CLAIMED` or `NEGATED` axis → every required proof-obligation row present exactly once.
- Active axis with zero required rows → schema error.
- Inactive axis with substantive obligation adjudication → schema error.
- `FAIL` with no canonical failure family → incomplete response.
- Non-empty failure family must be one of the enumerated canonical IDs.
- `NOT_APPLICABLE` is legal only for a conditional obligation and requires an applicability reason.
- Claim activity in the response must equal the canonical activity source.

Invalid responses fail validation before scoring; they are not converted into disagreement points.
